## Decisions and why

- **Player glass** (`#am-glass`, `glassStates.focused` in `theme.js`): fitted to macOS's own glass; the full write-up, data and tools live in their own repo, https://github.com/emribilemir/liquid-glass-research (moved out of `dev/glass`). Key points: Apple Music's player is an `NSGlassView` and renders like plain `NSGlassEffectView` regular; glass looks different in focused and background windows, and every measurement must be focused (borderless test windows cannot become key unless subclassed); the theme keeps only the focused look; the focused glass is nearly clear with a darken halo, a luminance tone (black 32, white 185), a soft rim fold drawn at device resolution, and a highlight along the top and bottom edges. Fit error 2.83 on lab patterns (was 21.9 for the hand-tuned glass) and 5.97 against frames of Apple Music scrolling.
- **Performance:** anything animating over the player re-runs its backdrop filter every frame. The progress bar and marquee move in device-pixel `steps()`; idle playback dropped from ~27% to ~1–5% of a core (`dev/client/cpu.mjs`). Keep new animations behind the player stepped or short.
- **Sidebar and page:** the sidebar is Apple's dark material, `#252525` at 76% over the blurred page, so about a quarter of the page shows through. The earlier "glass, ~15% darker" was measured over a dark page, where both read alike; over colour it turned the sidebar into the page's colour (red playlist headers, orange artwork), while Apple keeps a deep version of it (screenshots: page (112,77,52) gives sidebar (65,49,39)). The edge glow under it is therefore subtle, as on Apple Music (+6 to +14 per channel). The page runs under it: `.am-native-main` spans the `left-sidebar` column, `.main-view-container__scroll-node-child` is padded by `--am-side`, carries the `main-view-grid-area` size container and clips horizontal overflow. `#am-artist` fills the window; its hero and rows go under the glass. Sidebar rows use fixed points (32px rows, 16px insets, 18px icons, 11px section titles), as measured on Apple Music.
- **Fonts:** Spotify pins its own font on `<html>` for non-Latin locales (Turkish included) via `.spotify-mix-non-latin` with an `!important` no `:root` rule beats; the Inter stack is declared on `body`.
- **Artist pages:** routes are matched without the locale prefix; colour is sampled from the artwork (i.scdn.co allows CORS) with Spotify's extracted colour as a 600ms fallback; grey artwork keeps a grey page; artists without `visuals.headerImage` get the round portrait layout. Back/share/more are `no-drag` (they sit in the title-bar strip).
- **Toolbar row:** `.am-native-main` spans the `global-nav` row too, and `--am-page-top` (56px, shorter than the sidebar's 76px `--am-header`: Spotify's bars add their own top padding, and the full height left a gap the user found too wide) is `padding-top` on Spotify's page scroll viewport (`.main-view-container__scroll-node > [data-overlayscrollbars-viewport]`; OverlayScrollbars also wraps small scrollers inside pages, such as the playlist action bar, and padding those opened big gaps in playlists; `!important` over OverlayScrollbars' inline padding). Sticky positions respect the scroll container's padding, so the home chips bar and the track list header stop below the toolbar without moving them. The top colour layers (home: `main > div > [style^="background-color"]:first-child:has(~ [data-testid="home-page"])`, scoped to the home page because genre pages have a gradient layer in the same place, below their image header, which moved over the image; playlists/albums: the first child of `[data-testid="entity-header"]`, coloured by an inline `background-color` or, on newer playlist pages, an inline `--background-base`) reach up by `--am-page-top` (content-box padding), and so do the glass layers of the chips bar and `.main-topBar-background`, so toolbar and title bar read as one band once scrolled. `.main-topBar-container` moves to `top: --am-page-top` and loses Spotify's `contain: content`, which clipped its glass. A fixed `#global-nav-bar::before` shade (from the sidebar's edge) and faint fills on the buttons keep the controls readable. The library view starts at `--am-page-top`, below the toolbar's buttons: it is outside `.Root`'s stacking context, so at `top: 0` it covered the toolbar; `.am-layout` is painted `--am-bg` so the strip above it matches.
- **Top bars:** Spotify's scrolled bars (home chips row, playlist/album title bar, track list header) are the theme's glass (`#2a2a2a` at 62%, blur 30, saturate 1.8). The home chips layer is tagged on first scroll with `data-am-glass`; Spotify's 60% black `.main-topBar-overlay` is cleared.
- **Search stays a field:** Spotify puts `.searchInputCollapsed` on `#global-nav-bar` itself when the toolbar's centre gets narrow and folds the field to a 48px icon; user.css undoes that, so the field narrows with the toolbar (about 200px at an 800px window with a 360px sidebar).
- **Account row (bottom left):** notifications ("What's New"), Friend Activity and the account menu moved out of the toolbar at the user's request. The sidebar's `.am-account` row has the profile chip plus bell and friends buttons, which `.click()` Spotify's own toolbar buttons. The toolbar's trailing group stays rendered but `visibility:hidden`, fixed under that row, so Spotify's account menu (with Spicetify's mcloud, Marketplace and Spicy Lyrics entries) opens anchored there and panels open as usual; the theme's own profile popover is only the fallback when the native button is missing. "What's New" has no stable hook: it is the trailing button that is neither `[data-restore-focus-key]` nor `user-widget-link`, and it routes to `/content-feed`.
- **Player extras:** Spicy Lyrics popup (`SpicyLyrics_PopupLyricsButton`, a Document Picture-in-Picture window; hidden when Spicy is missing), lyrics, Connect, queue, volume. Spotify's Connect button has no test id and a translated label; it is found by `aria-describedby*="connect"` or as the button after the queue button, and its device list opens in the right panel as an `<aside>` labelled like the button, so it shares the queue's glass sheet.
- **Right panel:** Friend Activity and the queue are read from the panel itself (an `<aside>` labelled like the button with `data-restore-focus-key` `buddy_feed` / `queue`) and shown as one glass sheet, so any close button closes it. Spotify cross-fades panels: the outgoing `<aside>` (often the hidden Now Playing view) stays mounted beside the incoming one for ~300 ms, and React rewrites its `class`. The newest aside is current and gets `data-am-current` (an attribute survives the class rewrite); `user.css` hides the others. Aside swaps sync straight from the MutationObserver, not after the 150 ms debounce, so neither opening nor closing flashes Now Playing or the outgoing feed. Measured frame by frame in 1.3.6 over the DevTools port. While a sheet is open `#main` is lifted over `#am-library` and `.Root` goes transparent; Spotify fades `.Root`'s background over .5s, which showed as a grey flash over the library, so that rule also sets `transition:none`.
- **Sidebar on artist pages:** Apple Music's sidebar is a dark material letting ~15–25% of the page through; on a dark page that reads as "15% darker", but over an artist's colour it is about half the page's brightness (macOS 26 screenshot: page (112,77,52), sidebar (65,49,39)). `body.am-tinted #am-sidebar` mixes 34% of `--am-tint-top` into #161616 at 85% opacity, which lands on (66,49,35) for that page. The edge glow is off there: it turned a one-colour page orange.
- **Sidebar edge glow:** Apple Music's sidebar picks up the colours of the artwork beside it. `#am-sidebar-glow` (body-level, z 39, under the sidebar: inside it, the sidebar's own backdrop-filter would make it a backdrop root and hide the page) is a backdrop `blur(56px) saturate(3.5) brightness(1.25)` reaching 140px past the sidebar, masked to the sidebar and fading out ~160px in from the edge. Weaker blurs barely reached the edge (+4 levels); stronger saturation over a wide mask turned the whole sidebar the colour of playlist headers. Compared against a macOS 26 screenshot over coloured tiles (Search's "Browse all"). Scroll frame times on a 2x Mac: unchanged (p95 ~17ms with and without). The strong saturate/brightness blew out large coloured areas (Liked Songs' purple header under the sidebar went from blue 131 to 199 at the edge), so `url(#am-glow-cap)` ends the chain: identity up to 0.4, then a soft ceiling at ~0.66. Edge blue there is now 142; tile glow on Search barely changes (77 → 74).
- **Narrow sidebar:** the account row's profile chip has `min-width:0`, so at the 170px minimum it shrinks (the name ellipsizes) instead of pushing the bell and friends buttons out over the page.
- **Toasts:** Spotify's snackbar lives inside `#main`'s stacking context, under the floating player and fully under `#am-artist`, so "Bağlantı kopyalandı" after Share looked like Share did nothing. The theme's messages go through `notify()` (`.am-toast`, above the player, Liquid Glass like the player through its own lens `#am-glass-toast`: `glassLens()` draws one displacement map per element); Spotify's own toasts are lifted to `bottom: 84px`, which helps everywhere except over the artist view.
- **Player menus:** the artist — album line opens "Sanatçıya Git / Albüme Git"; the title goes to the album. The volume capsule follows Apple Music (opens after 0.5s on the speaker, mute keeps the level).
- **Windows "Transparency effects" off** reports `prefers-reduced-transparency`; the theme then shows solid surfaces with the glass rim. This is why some users saw a flat grey player.
- **macOS native Liquid Glass** cannot be used: Spotify's UI is a Chromium page, Spicetify only reaches its HTML/CSS/JS, and the native material is an AppKit view. Only Spotify could adopt it.

## Gotchas found the hard way

- `spicetify config <key> <value>` **writes**, it does not read, and the change can get applied right away. Read `config-xpui.ini` instead (path from `spicetify -c`). Remove a list item with `spicetify config extensions name-`. On this machine `extensions` should be empty (only the theme and Marketplace load).
- Spotify marks the window's top 60px (`.body-drag-top`) as a drag region; controls there need `-webkit-app-region:no-drag`. The browser preview cannot show this.
- Links inside Spotify carry a locale prefix (`/intl-tr/artist/…`); use `routePath()`.
- React rewrites `className` on some nodes while scrolling; tag those with a `data-` attribute.
- `overflow-clip-margin` accepts a plain length only (no `var()`/`clamp()`).
- `mix-blend-mode` on the player's children does not see the backdrop, so the rim lives inside the SVG filter.
- Selectors with a descendant combinator do not match nodes already removed from the DOM; checks on MutationObserver `removedNodes` need ancestor-free selectors.
- Inline-style selectors on Spotify nodes must be narrow: search page genre tiles also carry inline `background-color` and are `position:relative`.
- Spicy Lyrics mounts `#SpicyLyricsPage` in Spotify's scroll viewport (sibling of the padded content), sizes itself from its own width (`container-type:size`) and finds its host via `:is(.Root__main-view, :where(#main-view)) .main-view-container div[data-overlayscrollbars-viewport]`. Its lyrics button toggles on its own `active` flag.
- When reproducing Spotify states over the debug port, wait for extensions to load (Spicy's button appears a few seconds after start).
- `git fetch` to github.com sometimes times out from this machine; retry before pushing.
- On macOS the OS turns Shift+wheel into a horizontal wheel; a synthetic `deltaY` with Shift (Playwright, CDP) does not scroll rows there. Send `deltaX` instead.
- `<body>` overflows by ~8px horizontally (it is `overflow-x:hidden`, so users cannot scroll it), but `scrollIntoView` (and Playwright's actionability scrolling on `hover()`/`click()`) shifts the whole UI 8px left. Reset with `document.body.scrollLeft=0`; move the mouse by coordinates instead.
